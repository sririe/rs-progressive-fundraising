# Redstamp SOW Skill — Example SOWs

> **Purpose:** Reference examples of real, executed (or execution-ready) Statements of Work from Redstamp client engagements. These examples demonstrate how Redstamp adapts scope language, pricing, assumptions, and structure across different project types and budget levels.
>
> **Usage:** The SKILL.md should reference these examples when generating new SOWs. Match the closest example by engagement type and budget range, then adapt language and structure accordingly.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-03-25

---

## Pattern Summary (Read This First)

### Rate History
| Period | Rate | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| 2020–2022 | $140 USD/hr | Nitro, Reltio era |
| 2024–2026 | $150 USD/hr | SpecterOps, Terminal (standard) |
| 2025–2026 | $165 USD/hr | Onward Robotics (premium) |

### Signing Title Evolution
- 2020–2022: "Co-Founder and Creative Director"
- 2025–2026: "Co-Founder" (shorter, current standard)

### Risks Section Is Near-Boilerplate
The same 3–5 risks appear verbatim across almost every SOW:
1. SOW based on current knowledge; deliverables may shift during discovery
2. Client feedback delay causing project blockage
3. Budget/timeline impact from scope changes
4. Partner agency misalignment (when applicable)
5. Required materials delayed

Mitigation language is identical across all examples. The skill can safely template this section with minimal customization.

### Payment Structure Patterns
| Project Size | Structure | Example |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Small (<$10K) | 60% upfront / 40% completion | Terminal $8.5K |
| Medium ($10–30K) | 50/50 or phase-based | SpecterOps $17.5K, $28K |
| Large ($50K+) | Monthly billing or 100% upfront | Reltio $84K, Nitro $145K |

### Revision Rounds
- Standard: 1 round included
- Nitro (large brand project): 2 rounds included
- Always specify overage rate for additional rounds

### 48-Hour Feedback Turnaround
Standard across all examples — client feedback expected within 48 hours or timeline may shift.

### Resourcing Guarantee Window
Recent SOWs include a 2 business day resourcing guarantee from date SOW is sent. After this period, availability and timeline may need reassessment. This appears in SpecterOps and Onward examples.

## Example 1: Small Fixed-Fee — Creative Production

**Client:** Terminal Industries
**Project:** Animated Tradeshow Video
**Fee:** $8,500 USD | **Rate:** $150/hr | **Date:** January 2026

### Project Overview

Terminal Industries is a leading provider of yard management technology solutions. Their platform leverages advanced automation capabilities to help logistics operations optimize their yard workflows, reduce wait times, and improve overall efficiency.

Redstamp will produce an animated promotional video for Terminal Industries to be premiered at their upcoming tradeshow appearance. This video will illustrate Terminal's platform capabilities through an isometric yard visualization, showcasing key automation workflows and operational efficiencies. The video will be designed for booth display and digital distribution across marketing channels.

### Scope

The following deliverables are in-scope for this project.

**Pre-Production**
- Creative concept development and storyboarding
- Visual style exploration and direction (isometric illustration style)
- Script/narrative development for voiceover or text-based storytelling

**Production**
- Isometric yard illustration — custom environment depicting Terminal's operational context
- Character and vehicle asset creation
- Motion graphics and animation production (target duration: 60–90 seconds)

**Post-Production**
- Sound design and audio integration
- Final renders in formats optimized for:
  - Tradeshow booth display (large format)
  - Social media distribution (1:1, 9:16, 16:9)

### Tentative Timeline

| Deliverable | Target Date |
|:---|:---|
| Creative concept and storyboard approval | Week 1 |
| Illustration and asset development | Weeks 2–3 |
| Animation and motion graphics | Weeks 3–4 |
| Sound design and final delivery | Week 5 |

*Timeline is contingent on timely client feedback. All dates are estimates and subject to adjustment based on project requirements.*

### Assumptions

1. Client will provide all necessary brand assets, logos, and product screenshots at project kickoff.
2. Client will designate a single point of contact for feedback and approvals.
3. One (1) round of revisions is included per deliverable. Additional rounds will be billed at $150 USD per hour.
4. The project fee does not include stock music licensing, voiceover talent, or other third-party costs. These will be quoted separately if needed.
5. Final video length is targeted at 60–90 seconds. Significant changes to duration may require a scope adjustment.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|:---|:---|
| This SOW is based on current knowledge. As the discovery and strategy take place, deliverables may shift and change. | Redstamp PM to stay close on deliverables and estimates and flag any scope changes or additional requests ASAP for client alignment. |
| Client feedback delay: Should the Client's team be delayed in providing necessary feedback to Redstamp, this could cause a blockage on the project, where we would be unable to move forward until feedback is received. | Should there be delays on this front, the overall project timeline will be shifted. Redstamp offers regular check-in meetings and calls with our clients, and keeps a clear line of communication between our teams. |
| Impact on budget and timeline from scope changes | Redstamp offers regular check-in meetings and calls with our clients, and keeps a clear line of communication between our teams. |

### Out of Scope

- Live action video production
- Voiceover recording (can be quoted separately)
- Additional video lengths or formats beyond those specified
- Ongoing video updates or versioning post-delivery
- Print collateral design

### Project Terms

1) **Project Fee.** The Client shall pay Redstamp for the performance of the Services. A total of $8,500.00 USD. Any additional work required above the project fee will be calculated at the agency's hourly rate of $150 USD per hour (the "Agency Rate").

2) **Payment Schedule.**
   - 60% ($5,100 USD) upon execution of this Agreement; payment terms NET 7.
   - 40% ($3,400 USD) upon delivery of final assets; payment terms NET 30.

3) **Change Orders.** Redstamp's fees are based on the time it will take to complete the Services under normal circumstances. If the Client expands or alters a project's scope, requires further alterations to a project after sign-off or other factors arise such as multiple revision requests or delays on the Client's part in providing content or instructions that require more hours from Redstamp than are currently anticipated to complete a project, Redstamp will:
   a) advise you when our estimate for that project has been exceeded;
   b) outline the additional work required to complete the project along with the time it will take and the additional cost of completion calculated at the agency's hourly rate of $150 USD per hour (the "Agency Rate");
   c) provide a revised production schedule and estimated completion date; and
   d) ask you to confirm you want Redstamp to complete the additional work.

This SOW is valid for 30 days from the date of creation. This SOW is issued under a Services Agreement between Red Stamp Agency Inc. and Terminal Industries.

## Example 5: Retainer — On-Demand Support (No Monthly Cap)

**Client:** SpecterOps
**Project:** 2026 Website Maintenance and Support
**Rate:** $150/hr | **Date:** January 2026

### Project Overview

This Statement of Work outlines the terms for ongoing website maintenance and on-demand support for SpecterOps' WordPress-based web properties. Redstamp will provide responsive support for routine updates, troubleshooting, and minor enhancements on an as-needed basis.

### Scope

The following services are available as on-demand requests:
- WordPress core, plugin, and theme updates
- Security monitoring and patching
- Bug fixes and troubleshooting
- Minor content updates and page modifications
- Performance optimization
- Backup management and monitoring
- Minor design updates within existing templates

### Assumptions

1. Requests will be submitted via a dedicated Slack channel or email.
2. Redstamp will acknowledge requests within 1 business day and provide an estimated level of effort before proceeding.
3. Urgent/critical issues (site down, security breach) will be prioritized and addressed as soon as possible.
4. This agreement does not guarantee availability for large-scope projects — those will be scoped under a separate SOW.
5. Work will be billed in 30-minute increments.
6. If the website is placed on hold by the Client for more than 10 business days, Redstamp reserves the right to reallocate resources. A restart period of up to 5 business days may apply.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |

| Critical security vulnerabilities require immediate attention | Redstamp will prioritize critical security issues and communicate resolution timeline within 2 hours of identification. |

### Out of Scope

- Full website redesigns or major feature development
- Content creation or copywriting
- SEO strategy or implementation
- Third-party plugin development
- Hosting management (unless specifically requested)
