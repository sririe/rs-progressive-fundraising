# Where We Go From Here Deck Outline

This document captures the slide-level structure of the PowerPoint deck used in the March 13, 2026 client meeting so the presentation remains searchable and easy for future agents to reference.

Source deck: [2026-03-13-where-we-go-from-here-deck.pptx](/Users/spencer/projects-work/rs-progressive-fundraising/projects/gift-cards/docs/discovery/2026-03-13-where-we-go-from-here-deck.pptx)

## Slide Outline

1. **Where We Go From Here**
   Doug asked Red Stamp to come back with a clear recommendation. The deck positions one critical step as necessary before a responsible recommendation can be finalized.

2. **The Problem**
   Three current issues in Progressive's digital business:
   - single point of failure around one person, one machine, one set of scripts
   - fragile encrypted-email delivery
   - disconnected back-office invoicing

3. **Key Insight**
   Gift cards are cash equivalents. Any system touching card numbers should be treated like a financial system.

4. **Security Reality**
   Security, vendor complexity, URL delivery risk, and the insecurity of the current process are framed as the practical reasons not to rush into a build recommendation.

5. **Open Questions**
   Risk exposure, insurance coverage, vendor requirements, and prior incidents are listed as unanswered questions that materially affect the right solution.

6. **Why Discovery Has To Come First**
   The case against premature architecture: recommending a solution before validating workflows and risk boundaries would be guesswork.

7. **Phase 1: Structured Discovery**
   A half-day working session with Lloyd, led by Danny on site with Spencer remote, producing vendor runbooks, technical specification, and security assessment.

8. **Investment**
   The proposed discovery engagement:
   - half-day structured discovery session
   - Danny on site, Spencer remote, Lloyd and Mario participating
   - April 2026 timing in the deck
   - 40 hours total effort
   - $6,750 investment

9. **What Happens After Discovery**
   Discovery unlocks the recommendation and subsequent work on the card generation tool, order platform, and customer portal.

10. **Call To Action**
   The single action requested: schedule the Lloyd session.
