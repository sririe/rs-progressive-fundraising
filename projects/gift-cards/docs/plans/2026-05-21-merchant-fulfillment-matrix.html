Deeper context - merchant workflows

Merchant fulfillment matrix

This translates the observed merchant differences into three fulfillment models. The goal is to help the team scope the vault around repeatable patterns instead of treating every merchant as a one-off.

Pattern 1

Merchant-provided cards

Most merchants provide URLs, PDFs, codes, or card files. Progressive pulls from inventory, prepares a customer-ready file, encrypts it, and sends it.

Pattern 2

Progressive-generated PDFs

A smaller group provides raw card data. Progressive uses scripts and templates to generate branded PDFs, often followed by SystemOne URL creation.

Pattern 3

Walmart activation

Walmart is unique: cards are issued just in time through Walmart's activation tool, then generated, delivered, and reconciled.

Pattern 1: Merchant-provided cards

Examples What Progressive receives What staff do today Vault implication
Tim Hortons, Esso, Petro-Canada, Starbucks, Best Buy, Boston Pizza, Subway, Uber, Winners URLs, PDFs, card codes, challenge codes, or spreadsheet inventory depending on merchant. Pull inventory from merchant spreadsheets, paste into customer-ready templates, encrypt, and send. Inventory import, status tracking, customer-ready file creation, encryption, and delivery prep can share one workflow.

This is the majority path. The complexity is not PDF generation; it is merchant-by-merchant inventory shape, file format, and delivery preference.

Pattern 2: Progressive-generated PDFs

Examples What Progressive receives What staff do today Vault implication
Amazon, Loblaws, Shoppers Drug Mart, plus 1-2 to confirm Raw card numbers, PINs, claim codes, or inventory files that need to become customer-ready card assets. Populate merchant workbooks, run Lloyd's Python/Inkscape scripts, generate PDFs, zip files, and sometimes upload to SystemOne. This is where secure card-data handling matters most. The vault must track raw card data, generated PDFs, job status, and cleanup rules.

The "~5 merchants" count is based on discovery and should be confirmed against Lloyd's folders and templates before pricing is final.

Pattern 3: Walmart

Step What happens Why it is different Vault implication
Activation Progressive uses Walmart's virtual gift card activation tool to issue numbers and PINs just in time. There is no pre-purchased digital inventory to pull from. Walmart should be treated as its own module, not just another merchant row.
PDF generation Generated card numbers are turned into Walmart e-card PDFs across denomination tabs. The flow includes Lloyd's PowerShell and PDF generation pieces. Requires a reproduction pass before the team can confidently price automation depth.
Reconciliation Monthly sheets track first and last card numbers for settlement with Walmart. This creates a reporting obligation beyond simple delivery. Reconciliation can be designed into the vault, but may not be a V1 blocker.
