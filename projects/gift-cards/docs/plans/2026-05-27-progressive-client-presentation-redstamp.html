The practical risk

The workflow works because specific people know how to run it.

That is the point of fragility. If volume grows, a key person is away, a script fails, a merchant changes its format, or an order needs support later, Progressive does not have one controlled place to see what happened.

Person-dependent

Process knowledge lives with specific people, not in the system.

Folder-dependent

The workflow relies on the right local and Drive folder structure.

Script-dependent

Some merchants depend on scripts and templates that are hard for staff to support.

Email-dependent

Delivery history and support context are split across email and generated files.

Presenter note

This is the risk framing. Consider adding a simple “what happens if Mario or Lloyd is unavailable?” question during the live walkthrough, but avoid making it dramatic.

Merchant fulfillment reality

The system should support three different merchant patterns.

Merchant-provided digital inventory

Most merchants provide usable card details: URLs, PDFs, card numbers, PINs, access codes, or spreadsheet inventory. Progressive pulls from inventory, prepares the customer-ready file, and delivers it.

Progressive-generated PDFs

A smaller group, including Amazon, Loblaws, and Shoppers Drug Mart, requires Progressive to generate card PDFs from raw card data.

Walmart

Walmart is a separate just-in-time activation flow with its own activation tool, generated PDFs, and monthly reconciliation requirements.

Phase 1 boundary

The first build should stay focused on internal fulfillment.

Include in Phase 1

  • Website order handoff into pending vault requests, or an agreed fallback intake method, plus manual/admin order creation.
  • Fulfillment dashboard, request list, request detail, open/closed status, and payment-status tracking.
  • Digital Card Vault with CSV import, merchant/denomination inventory counts, allocation, and status.
  • Customer-ready CSV/export creation once inventory is allocated.
  • Order activity history for key actions: created, paid, allocated, exported, closed, deleted, or returned to inventory.
  • Production security foundation: authenticated access, roles, encrypted card data, backups, logging, and separate staging/production environments.

Planned for Phase 2 and Phase 3

  • Full customer portal or customer account system.
  • Replacing the entire public ordering experience.
  • QuickBooks invoice creation or accounting integration.
  • Direct recipient delivery as a managed service.
  • Secure delivery portal, download links, and customer access logging.
  • Automated delivery emails from the vault.
  • Direct merchant integrations or automatic card issuing.
  • Automation of third-party merchant websites, activation portals, or login-based merchant workflows.
  • Walmart monthly reconciliation automation.
  • Full physical card warehouse/pick-pack workflow.
  • Redemption reporting where merchant data is not available.
  • Merchant-branded portals.
  • Additional merchant formats or file-creation rules beyond the agreed Phase 1 set.
