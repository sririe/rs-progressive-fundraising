Model & technical detail

The model. Progressive's spreadsheets are just how they store cards today. The vault normalizes on ingest and owns one record. Credential reduces to url + typed secrets. Output is set per order. Walmart is the only two-way output.

Card normalization — every merchant we know of

23 merchants, ~8 raw layouts, collapse to 7 card families → one record. The importer is configurable across the families. The bounded commitment is the active-merchant set below. A card format we haven't mapped is a change order.
