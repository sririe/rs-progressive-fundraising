## <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Project Overview</span>

<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Progressive and Redstamp have worked together through several phases of the business — from the original operational assessment through the commerce integration that replaced phone and fax ordering with a system built around your margins.</span>

<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">In our recent conversations, one thing has become clear: the digital gift card workflow is where the most concentrated risk and the most immediate growth constraint sit today. The fulfillment process runs through one person, one set of scripts, and a manual pipeline that works at current volume but cannot scale. If digital order volume approaches what you're doing on the physical side, the current process can't keep up — and the security posture around card data needs to be understood before any new system is designed.</span>

<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">This discovery engagement is a focused effort to document exactly how that workflow operates today, assess where the real risks are, and come back with a recommendation Progressive can act on. It is not a commitment to build — it is the step that ensures whatever gets built next is grounded in how things actually work, not assumptions.</span>

<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">We've also heard clearly that stickering and invoicing are real operational pain points. This engagement will capture a high-level view of those areas so we can advise on what should be tackled next and in what order, without letting the session become a broad operations audit.</span>

## <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Scope</span>

<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">The following deliverables are in-scope for this engagement.</span>

**<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">On-Site Discovery Session (Half-Day)</span>**

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Structured walkthrough of the current digital gift card fulfillment workflow, vendor by vendor</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Live observation of tools, scripts, spreadsheets, portals, and manual steps involved in card generation and delivery</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Documentation of where each workflow differs by vendor (Walmart, Amazon, Loblaws, Chapters, and any additional active vendors)</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Identification of edge cases, exceptions, and points where the process is slow, brittle, or error-prone</span>

**<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Security and Data Flow Assessment</span>**

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Mapping of where sensitive card data exists at each stage of the process — intake, generation, storage, and delivery</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Assessment of current access controls, transfer methods, and storage practices</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Identification of security boundaries and risk areas that must inform the design of any future system</span>

**<span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Operator Readiness Assessment</span>**

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Evaluation of how much of the current workflow lives in undocumented knowledge versus structured process</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Assessment of current backup operator (Mario) independence and readiness</span>

* <span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">Identification of what would need to be simplified or documented for broader staff use</span>

* <span data-proof="suggestion" data-id="m1774479030875_6" data-by="ai:claude" data-kind="replace"><span data-proof="authored" data-by="ai:claude">High-level capture of invoicing and administrative process friction</span></span>
