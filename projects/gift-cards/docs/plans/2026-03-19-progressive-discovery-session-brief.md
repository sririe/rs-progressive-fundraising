# Progressive Discovery Session Brief

## Session Purpose

This session is designed to help us understand the current digital gift card workflow in enough detail to make a strong recommendation about what Progressive should do next.

Our focus is on seeing the real process, understanding where risk and friction sit today, and identifying the best next step for a secure and scalable future workflow.

## What We Plan To Cover

### 1. Current digital fulfillment workflow

- How digital card data is received
- How inventory is stored and tracked today
- What steps are taken when an order comes in
- What differs by vendor
- Which tools, scripts, spreadsheets, or portals are involved

### 2. Security and handoffs

- Where sensitive card data lives during the process
- How files, URLs, or outputs are stored and transferred
- Which steps depend on specific people or undocumented knowledge

### 3. Operator readiness

- What is straightforward today
- What is difficult to hand off
- What would need to be simplified or documented for broader staff use

### 4. Delivery expectations

- What customers are actually asking for
- Which turnaround expectations matter most
- Whether there are important exceptions by customer or vendor

### 5. Adjacent operational inputs

If time allows, we would also like a high-level look at:

- physical-card stickering
- invoicing and admin workflow friction

These are helpful context areas, but the main focus for the session remains digital fulfillment.

## What Would Be Helpful To Have Ready

- A list of active digital vendors
- One or two representative workflows ready to walk through live
- Sample inputs and outputs where possible
- Any current spreadsheets, checklists, scripts, portals, or templates that are part of the process
- A quick explanation of where digital inventory comes from today
- Clarity on any constraints around screenshots, recording, or sharing files for documentation purposes

## Suggested Session Flow


| Time      | Topic                                                                                 |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00-0:15 | Framing, goals, and overview of the current digital business                          |
| 0:15-2:15 | Live walkthrough of the digital workflow, including vendor differences and real tools |
| 2:15-2:45 | Security, storage, access, and handoff discussion                                     |
| 2:45-3:15 | Operator readiness and transferability discussion                                     |
| 3:15-3:45 | High-level look at adjacent pain points, including stickering and invoicing           |
| 3:45-4:00 | Wrap-up, open questions, and next steps                                               |


## What We Hope To Leave With

- A clear picture of the current-state workflow
- Notes on where the biggest risks and bottlenecks sit today
- Better understanding of which parts of the process vary by vendor
- Enough detail to recommend what should be tackled next and what should wait

## Working Notes

- We do not need the session to answer every long-term product question.
- We do want it to replace the biggest assumptions with something concrete.
- If there are areas that need follow-up afterward, we will capture those clearly at the end of the session.
