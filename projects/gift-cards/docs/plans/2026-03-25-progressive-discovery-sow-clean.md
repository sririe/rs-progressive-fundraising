## Project Overview

Progressive and Redstamp have worked together through several phases of the business — from the original operational assessment through the commerce integration that replaced phone and fax ordering with a system built around your margins.

In our recent conversations, one thing has become clear: the digital gift card workflow is where the most concentrated risk and the most immediate growth constraint sit today. The fulfillment process runs through one person, one set of scripts, and a manual pipeline that works at current volume but cannot scale. If digital order volume approaches what you're doing on the physical side, the current process can't keep up — and the security posture around card data needs to be understood before any new system is designed.

This discovery engagement is a focused effort to document exactly how that workflow operates today, assess where the real risks are, and come back with a recommendation Progressive can act on. It is not a commitment to build — it is the step that ensures whatever gets built next is grounded in how things actually work, not assumptions.

We've also heard clearly that stickering and invoicing are real operational pain points. This engagement will capture a high-level view of those areas so we can advise on what should be tackled next and in what order, without letting the session become a broad operations audit.

## Scope

The following deliverables are in-scope for this engagement, informed by a half-day on-site discovery session with Progressive's team.

**Vendor Runbooks**

- Step-by-step documentation of each vendor's digital fulfillment workflow, written for operators rather than developers
- Produced from a structured vendor-by-vendor walkthrough of the current process, including live observation of tools, scripts, spreadsheets, and portals
- Covers where each workflow differs by vendor (Walmart, Amazon, Loblaws, Chapters, and any additional active vendors)
- Identifies edge cases, exceptions, and points where the process is slow, brittle, or error-prone

**Technical Specification**

- Informed by the real workflow, covering what a future card generation system would need to handle
- Includes an assessment of operator readiness — how much lives in undocumented knowledge, current backup operator (Mario) independence, and what would need to be simplified for broader staff use

**Security and Handoff Assessment**

- Mapping of where sensitive card data exists at each stage — intake, generation, storage, and delivery
- Assessment of current access controls, transfer methods, and storage practices
- Identification of security boundaries and risk areas that must inform the design of any future system

**Strategic Roadmap**

- Prioritized recommendation for what Progressive should build next, what should wait, and the rationale behind the sequencing
- Informed by all findings from the discovery session

**Adjacent Operational Inputs (Observational)**

- High-level observation of the physical-card stickering workflow and invoicing friction while on-site
- These areas will be noted to inform future engagement planning but do not produce standalone deliverables

## Assumptions

1. Progressive will make Mario available for the full on-site discovery session, with Doug available for framing and closeout. Lloyd will be available for approximately one (1) hour during the session to walk through key elements of the digital fulfillment workflow. Given the limited time with Lloyd, follow-up conversations may be required after the on-site session to clarify workflow details, cover gaps, or validate documentation. These follow-up sessions are included within the project scope.
2. Progressive will provide access to the tools, scripts, spreadsheets, and portals used in the current digital fulfillment workflow during the session.
3. Progressive will provide a list of active digital vendors and representative sample inputs/outputs ahead of the session as prework.
4. Client will confirm any constraints around screenshots, screen recording, or sharing of files for documentation purposes before the session.
5. One (1) round of revisions is included on final deliverables. Additional rounds will be billed at $150 CAD per hour.
6. This engagement is scoped as a discovery and assessment. Recommendations produced will inform a separate Statement of Work for any subsequent build phase. This SOW does not commit either party to a follow-on engagement.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|:---|:---|
| Key staff unavailability during the discovery session could result in incomplete workflow documentation. | Confirm attendee availability during scheduling. If Lloyd or Mario cannot attend a portion of the session, identify the gap and schedule a focused follow-up. |
| The on-site session may surface workflow complexity or vendor-specific edge cases that require additional investigation beyond the scoped effort. | Redstamp will flag any areas that exceed the discovery scope and recommend whether to address them within this engagement or defer to a subsequent phase. |
| Adjacent operational topics (stickering, invoicing) could expand the session focus beyond digital fulfillment if not managed carefully. | The session structure keeps digital fulfillment as the primary focus. Adjacent topics are captured as bounded inputs during a dedicated but time-limited segment. |

## Out of Scope

- Design or development of any software, tools, or systems
- Migration or modification of existing scripts, spreadsheets, or processes
- Full operational transformation or process re-engineering beyond the digital fulfillment workflow
- Detailed scoping or pricing of subsequent build phases (these will be addressed in a separate SOW informed by this engagement's findings)
- End-recipient digital delivery architecture or implementation
- Integration with merchant platforms, payment systems, or QuickBooks
- Ongoing support or maintenance
