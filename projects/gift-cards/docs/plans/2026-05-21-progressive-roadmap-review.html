Internal alignment - May 21, 2026

Progressive recommendation review

A team-facing review of the proposed direction after discovery: lead with a secure card vault, then sequence the customer portal and service expansion work from that foundation.

The recommendation

Build the secure card vault first. Discovery surfaced a lot of moving pieces: website orders, QuickBooks invoices, payment confirmation, merchant spreadsheets, Lloyd's scripts, SystemOne, Gmail delivery, and customer support questions. The vault solves the biggest risks and gaps in that workflow, and it can be delivered as a standalone project without replacing the ordering site, BenjaPay, QuickBooks, or merchant systems.

What changes for the business

Current state Future state Business impact
Paid digital orders are triggered by paper, email, and operator memory. Paid orders appear in a fulfillment queue with clear status. Staff can see what needs to be fulfilled without relying on one person's desk process.
Digital inventory lives across merchant spreadsheets, folders, and generated files. Cards are tracked by merchant, denomination, source, and fulfillment status. Fewer mistakes when pulling card numbers, PINs, URLs, or generated PDFs for an order.
Merchant-specific steps live in scripts, spreadsheets, and memory. The tool guides staff through the right steps for each merchant. Less guesswork across URL pulls, PDF generation, Walmart activation, and delivery prep.
When a script fails, the team has to reconstruct what happened from files and memory. Each order keeps a record of what was created, sent, skipped, or failed. Support does not depend on Lloyd's memory, screen shares, or guesswork.
Customers receive card files by email, with limited self-serve history. The vault creates a cleaner foundation for portal access and order history. Progressive can improve the client experience without rebuilding the fulfillment process later.

Decisions to align on

Security

Where does card data live?

Default recommendation: Secure Web Vault with clear decisions on who can see card data, how long files stay in the system, and what order activity gets recorded.

Scope

What is not in V1?

No customer portal, QuickBooks integration, direct-to-recipient delivery, or SystemOne replacement in the first build.

Expansion

What waits for evidence?

Walmart automation, redemption reporting, white-label portals, and stickering automation should be sequenced after the foundation.
