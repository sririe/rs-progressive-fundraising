# Progressive Fundraising — Secure Card Vault

## SUPPORT & MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT (Statement of Work)

Delivered to: Doug Beers
Client: Progressive Fundraising
Date: [July 2026]

## 01. Overview

This Statement of Work covers ongoing support and maintenance of the Secure Card Vault and Fulfillment Tool following Phase 1 launch. Its purpose is to give Progressive a known, predictable monthly cost for keeping the vault healthy, secure, and current, including the routine addition of new merchants, so day-to-day operation never depends on ad hoc requests or change orders.

This agreement begins upon Phase 1 launch and runs month-to-month alongside the vault. It is issued under the Services Agreement between Red Stamp Agency Inc. and Progressive Fundraising Inc. dated February 21, 2024.

## 02. Scope

### Ongoing monitoring & maintenance (included every month)

- Application and database monitoring (uptime, errors, performance).
- Security patching and dependency updates for the application stack.
- Backup monitoring and periodic restore verification.
- Hosting environment management (the underlying hosting cost itself remains a third-party pass-through, per the Phase 1 SOW).

### Support (drawn from included hours)

- Responding to questions from Progressive staff about vault operation.
- Investigating and fixing defects.
- Small workflow, label, or configuration adjustments.
- Assistance with import or export edge cases (unexpected file variants, allocation questions, re-exports).

### New-merchant additions & format changes (drawn from included hours)

- Adding a new merchant whose cards fit an existing credential pattern: configuring the merchant, mapping its inventory file, and running an end-to-end validation test before the merchant goes live. Typical effort is 2–4 hours per merchant.
- Absorbing supplier file-format changes for existing merchants (column or layout changes) through the import mapping.
- A merchant introducing a genuinely new credential type that no existing pattern covers remains a change order under the Phase 1 SOW terms.

### Peak-season coverage (November 1 – December 31)

- Priority response during Progressive's peak fulfillment season: support requests acknowledged same business day, with critical issues (system unavailable, fulfillment blocked) worked as the first priority until resolved.

## 03. Defect Coverage

Functional deficiencies identified within the warranty period defined in the Services Agreement (15 business days following delivery) are corrected at no charge and do not draw against included hours.

After the warranty period, fixes for defects that prevent the system from meeting the documented Phase 1 scope are handled under this agreement and drawn from the included monthly hours — Progressive does not pay separately for bug fixes, and defect fixes are never treated as change orders.

## 04. Assumptions

1. Requests are submitted through the agreed channel (email or shared Slack channel), acknowledged within one business day (same business day during peak season).
2. Redstamp provides an estimated level of effort before proceeding with any request expected to exceed the included monthly hours.
3. Included hours cover the scope areas above; unused hours do not roll over.
4. Larger enhancements (new features, new workflows, and Phase 2 items such as automated delivery emails or a customer portal) are scoped separately as a change order or new SOW.
5. Work is billed in 30-minute increments.
6. Third-party pass-through costs (hosting, licenses) are billed separately and are not part of the monthly fee.

## 05. Out of Scope

- New features or workflow changes beyond small adjustments.
- New credential types (change order, per the Phase 1 SOW).
- Automation of third-party merchant websites, activation portals, or the Walmart/Fiserv activation itself.
- QuickBooks, payment processing, or customer-facing ordering changes.
- Training beyond the Phase 1 handoff, unless requested (billable from included hours).

## 06. Terms

**1) Monthly Fee.** **$1,280 CAD plus tax per month**, which includes up to **8 hours** of support and maintenance effort as scoped above. 

**2) Additional Hours.** Effort beyond the included monthly hours, approved by Progressive in advance, is billed at the agency rate of **$160 CAD plus tax per hour** at month end (NET 30).

**3) Invoicing.** The monthly fee is invoiced on the 1st of each month, NET 30. Quarterly invoicing ($3,840 CAD plus tax per quarter) is available on request.

**4) Term & Termination.** The agreement begins upon Phase 1 launch and continues month-to-month. Either party may terminate with written notice, effective no earlier than the last day of the month following the month in which notice is given.

**5) Precedence.** This SOW is issued under the Services Agreement dated February 21, 2024; the Services Agreement's terms (including IP ownership, confidentiality, warranty, and liability) govern.
